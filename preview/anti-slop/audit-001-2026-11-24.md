# antislop audit 001: preview site

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Scope:** `preview/index.html` (homepage and all 8 views), checked at 390px and 1280px
- **Mode:** After. Nothing is changed until the team picks finding numbers.
- **Design Read:** B2B consulting site for enterprise data and finance leaders, in a soft glass "Ocean" language set by the team. Dials as built: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.
- **Direction used:** the design rules in `CLAUDE.md` (Ocean palette, Sora + Inter, glass/blur surfaces, rounded shapes). There is no separate `DESIGN.md`.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

---

## HIGH

**1. Teal text is too faint to read (R-25).**
Teal `#10b9a6` on white measures 2.47:1, and 2.28:1 on the page background. Small text needs 4.5:1. It is used for the client names under industry cards and results ("Arab Bank, 600+ branches", "Lupin"), "Layer 01", "Governed end to end" and the small chevrons in the stack.
*Fix:* use the darker teal `#0e7490` for small text (5.36:1), and keep the light teal for large text and decoration.

**2. White text on light gradients fails contrast (R-25).**
- The "Talk to an expert" buttons fade from dark teal to light teal; white text on the light end is 2.47:1.
- The featured service card ("Agentic AI and automation") ends at `#0f939a`; white body text there is 3.71:1.
- The coral end of the hero headline ("work.") is 2.82:1 on white; large text needs 3:1.

*Fix:* solid dark teal behind white text (5.36:1), and a slightly deeper coral stop in the hero headline.

**3. Small tap targets on phones (R-03).**
Breadcrumb links are 22px tall and footer links and the email link 19px. The minimum is 44px.
*Fix:* add padding to those links so each tap area is at least 44px tall, without changing how they look.

**4. Motion runs forever with no pause button (R-39).**
These loop endlessly:
- the platform strip under the hero;
- the hero rings;
- the hero agent console;
- several floating and spinning decorations (12 infinite animations in all).

The strip pauses only on mouse hover, which touch and keyboard users cannot do. Reduced-motion settings are respected, which is good.
*Fix:* add one small "Pause motion" button, and stop purely decorative loops after one cycle.

**5. Footer "Sitemap · Privacy" looks like links but goes nowhere (R-26, R-38).**
It is plain text. There is no privacy page on the site.
*Fix:* remove it, or the team supplies a privacy policy text to link to. (Needs a team decision: do not invent a policy.)

**6. IBM badge image (R-40, plus a visible bug).**
Both badges load from the old veracitiz.com site, have no `height`, and the one in "Who we are" currently shows blank.
*Fix:* needs the badge file from the team; then it is stored in `assets/` with width and height.

**7. Em dashes in site copy (R-02).**
44 em dashes appear in visible text (for example "AI agents need — then design").
This is the team's own copy, so the rule says to ask: keep them, or replace each with a comma, colon, full stop or brackets?

**8. Statistics and testimonials need a confirmed source (R-17, R-18, R-36).**
Nothing here looks invented, but the rules need each figure to have a real source:
- Results: 75%, 72 to 85%, 35%, 600+, 12 days.
- Company stats: 150+, 300+ man-years.
- The three quotes.

The quotes use coloured gradient circles where a photo would be, which reads as a fake avatar.
*Fix:* the team confirms the figures and quote permissions; replace the gradient circles with initials or the company name.

**9. "1st" is styled as a statistic but is not one (R-17, C-5).**
The second insight card shows a big "1st" next to the sentence "Agents without governance... accelerate it." It reads as a ranking claim with no source.
*Fix:* replace "1st" with a short word that fits (for example "Trust first") or drop the number styling for that card.

## MEDIUM

**10. The design rules themselves ask for glass/blur everywhere (R-10, R-37).**
Blur is on 7 surfaces (menu bar, dropdowns, agent console, cards and others); the dose cap is 1 or 2. `CLAUDE.md` asks for "glass/blur surfaces", so the rule says to ask the owner: keep glass as the house style, or limit it to the menu bar and the agent console?

**11. Same large shadow on almost everything (R-12).**
`--shadow` is used 23 times, so most cards float at the same height.
*Fix:* keep the shadow for raised things (menu bar, dropdowns, agent console) and use a thin border for cards in the page.

**12. Identical cards (R-14).**
- The six service cards share one layout, though one featured card breaks the pattern.
- The four product groups on each platform view are identical. The site rules require platform views to share one layout, so only the inner cards are in scope.

*Fix:* vary the service grid by importance (for example, the featured card plus a compact list for the rest).

**13. Generic line icons (R-04).**
Service and product cards use thin rounded line icons in the common library style, including a star for "AI and agents".
*Fix:* drop the icons (the headings already say it) or draw a small set specific to Veracitiz.

**14. Gradients used by default (R-01).**
Apart from the hero headline (kept on purpose), gradients fill:
- the main button;
- the featured card;
- the quote avatars;
- the small round markers in the stack.

*Fix:* solid colours there; this also fixes findings 2 and 8.

## LOW

**15. Buzzwords (R-16).**
- "AI-powered" (IBM Planning Analytics description).
- "Intelligent IT operations" (twice).
- "Enterprise-grade delivery".
- "Empowering lives, beyond the work".

"Robust" inside a client quote is left alone.
*Fix:* plainer words, for example "IT operations with AIOps", "Delivery for large enterprises", "Giving back, beyond the work". Wording is the team's call.

**16. Pill shape on everything (R-11).**
`999px` radius is used 28 times: buttons, chips, the platform strip, tabs and the status chip.
*Fix:* keep pills for buttons and tags; use the card radius for the platform strip items and tabs.

**17. Platform strip directly under the hero (R-05).**
It sits where a "Trusted by" logo bar usually goes. It lists platforms, not clients, so it is honest, but it repeats the "Platforms we deliver on" block lower down.
*Fix:* remove the strip, or keep it static (no scrolling), which also helps finding 4.

- **R-28:** the FAQ questions are specific to Veracitiz (agentic AI, data foundation, IBM partnership, platforms, locations).
- **R-05:** the "How we work" steps are four real stages, not a template three; the footer columns are Services, Platforms, Industries, Company, not the template set.

- **R-34:** no theme toggle is shipped, so there is no second mode to break.
- **R-03:** no page is wider than a 390px phone.
- **Liveliness:** the agent-run console is a clear focal point and a real identity motif (it appears in the hero and the agents section).
